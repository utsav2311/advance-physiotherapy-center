Structured Clinical Care

Our 4-Step Patient Recovery Journey

A structured, evidence-based physical therapy pathway designed to guide you safely from acute pain relief to complete functional independence.

01

1. Comprehensive Assessment

Your journey begins with an in-depth 1-on-1 evaluation by Dr. Shahrukh Firoz. We evaluate joint range of motion, muscle strength grading (MMT), sensory-motor reflexes, postural alignments, and review your diagnostic MRI/X-ray scans to pinpoint the anatomical source of pain.

02

2. Tailored Protocol Design

We formulate an individual treatment plan combining targeted manual therapy glides, directional preference exercises, calibrated electrotherapy frequencies (IFT/TENS/Ultrasound), and decompression traction based on whether your condition is acute, sub-acute, or chronic.

03

3. Supervised 1-on-1 Therapy

Every therapy session is conducted under direct clinical supervision. We ensure precise exercise execution, proper breathing mechanics, and continuous pain threshold monitoring to rebuild joint stability without aggravating injured tissues.

04

4. Long-Term Prevention

Recovery extends beyond the clinic doors. You receive a personalized home exercise program, workplace ergonomic sitting recommendations, and postural self-care techniques to ensure lasting recovery and prevent future pain flare-ups.
