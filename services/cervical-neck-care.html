Cervical Spine & Neck Care

With modern smartphone usage, prolonged laptop work, and age-related wear, cervical spine disorders have become extremely prevalent. Dr. Shahrukh Firoz provides specialized cervical therapy targeting cervical spondylosis, disc bulges (C5-C6 / C6-C7), trapped cervical nerve roots (cervical radiculopathy), and chronic upper trapezius spasms. Our multi-modal clinical approach integrates Maitland posteroanterior cervical mobilizations, therapeutic ultrasound, gentle motorized cervical traction, and deep neck flexor (longus colli/capitis) activation.

Understanding the Condition & Biomechanics

The cervical spine supports the full weight of the human head (approx. 5 kg in neutral posture). When the head tilts forward by just 45 degrees during smartphone or desk work, the effective load on cervical discs escalates to over 22 kg ('Tech Neck'). Over time, this causes disc dehydration, osteophyte (bone spur) formation, narrowing of nerve canals, and severe reflex spasm of the suboccipital and levator scapulae muscles, frequently triggering cervicogenic tension headaches and arm numbness.

Common Symptoms & Clinical Presentations

  • ⚠️Persistent neck stiffness, grinding sensations (crepitus), and restricted rotation while driving
  • ⚠️Cervical Radiculopathy: Numbness, tingling, or weakness radiating into the shoulder, elbow, or fingertips
  • ⚠️Cervicogenic Headaches: Throbbing pain originating at the base of the skull radiating to the forehead or eyes
  • ⚠️Chronic tension knots in the upper trapezius, levator scapulae, and rhomboid muscles
  • ⚠️Forward head posture and hunched cervical-thoracic junction

Evidence-Based Treatment Modalities

  • ✓Maitland Passive Cervical Mobilizations: Precise manual joint glides to restore intervertebral mobility and reduce facet irritation
  • ✓Deep Neck Flexor (DNF) Retraining: Craniocervical flexion exercises using biofeedback to reactivate deep spinal stabilizers
  • ✓Motorized Cervical Decompression Traction: Gentle axial unloading to widen neural foramina and relieve nerve root pressure
  • ✓Therapeutic Ultrasound & High-Frequency TENS: Accelerating localized microcirculation and breaking the pain-spasm cycle
  • ✓Suboccipital Myofascial Release: Manual soft-tissue release of tight muscles at the skull base to eliminate tension headaches
  • ✓Postural & Workstation Ergonomics: Screen height adjustments, sleep pillow recommendations, and hourly neck relief micro-drills

Phased Recovery Pathway

Phase 1: Pain Relief & Neural Decompression

Alleviating acute nerve root pinching and headache pressure using gentle traction, IFT, and suboccipital release.

Phase 2: Joint Gliding & Muscle Relaxation

Restoring cervical rotation and lateral flexion through Maitland mobilizations and active-assisted neck stretches.

Phase 3: Deep Stabilizer Retraining

Strengthening longus colli and longus capitis to maintain natural cervical lordosis and prevent compensatory superficial muscle tightness.

Phase 4: Functional Postural Resilience

Workstation ergonomic corrections, scapular retractor strengthening, and long-term cervical care routines.
