Specialized Physiotherapy Services

Modern, evidence-based treatments customized to your diagnosis, pain level, and recovery goals.

Back & Neck Pain Relief

Targeted therapy for cervical spondylosis, lumbar disc bulge, sciatica, pinched nerves, and acute or chronic lower back stiffness.

  • Spinal decompression & traction
  • Sciatica nerve glide therapy
  • Core & postural muscle strengthening

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Accelerated recovery programs for athletes and active individuals with ligament tears, muscle sprains, runner's knee, and tennis elbow.

  • ACL/MCL ligament rehab
  • Functional return-to-sport training
  • Kinesiology taping & mobility drills

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Carefully structured progressive rehabilitation following total knee/hip replacement, fracture fixation, and spine surgeries.

  • Safe joint mobilization
  • Scar tissue management & gait training
  • Restoring full range of motion

Joint & Arthritis Pain

Non-invasive management for knee osteoarthritis, frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), hip stiffness, and degenerative joint conditions.

  • Shoulder capsule stretching
  • Knee joint lubrication exercises
  • Pain relief without excess medication

Advanced Electrotherapy

State-of-the-art modalities including Interferential Therapy (IFT), TENS, Therapeutic Ultrasound, and Deep Heat for rapid pain reduction.

  • IFT & TENS pain gate stimulation
  • Deep tissue ultrasound healing
  • Reduces inflammation and swelling

Manual Therapy & Myofascial Release

Hands-on joint mobilization, soft tissue release, and muscle energy techniques to release stubborn muscle knots and restore alignment.

  • Myofascial trigger point release
  • Passive & active joint mobilization
  • Immediate tension relief

Posture Correction & Ergonomics

Corrective programs for tech-neck, rounded shoulders, kyphosis, and work-from-home repetitive strain injuries.

  • Comprehensive posture analysis
  • Ergonomic workplace guidance
  • Core & back stabilizer activation

Neurological Rehabilitation

Dedicated functional physical therapy for stroke recovery, Bell's Palsy facial paralysis, Parkinson's disease, and balance disorders.

  • Neuro-muscular re-education
  • Gait & balance coordination
  • Facial nerve stimulation
