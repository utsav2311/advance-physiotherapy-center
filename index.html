Our 4-Step Recovery Pathway

A systematic, clinical approach to help you transition from acute pain to complete functional independence.

01

Detailed Evaluation

02

Targeted Care Plan

Patient Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you prepare for your consultation and understand our recovery protocols.

No doctor's referral is required. You can directly consult Dr. Shahrukh Firoz (B.P.T., M.P.T.) for a primary clinical assessment, spinal evaluation, and personalized therapy program.

Please bring any previous medical prescriptions, X-rays, MRI scans, or hospital discharge summaries if available. We recommend wearing comfortable, loose clothing that allows easy movement of the affected joint.

Most acute pain conditions show noticeable relief within 3 to 5 regular sessions. For chronic conditions, spinal disc compression, or post-operative rehabilitation, Dr. Shahrukh will outline an honest recovery timeline during your initial assessment.
